- License file: `LICENSE-LUCIDE`

The SVG was adapted for Vektra's 16x16 icon grid: the viewBox was changed to
`0 0 16 16`, the outlined stroke was changed to `1.2px`, and the geometry was

of the upstream Lucide file.

Vektra does not vendor the full Lucide icon set.
